Kaichuan Qi

Welcome! My name is Kaichuan Qi (戚铠川). I am a graduate student in the math department at Penn State University, supervised by Prof. Ping Xu and Prof. Mathieu Stiénon.

Prior to Penn State, I obtained my master’s degree from KU Leuven, under the guidance of Prof. Marco Zambon. Before that, I did my bachelor’s in CUHK(Shenzhen).
